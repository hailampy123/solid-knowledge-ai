# Orcas: An Overview

Orcas (*Orcinus orca*), commonly called killer whales, are the largest members
of the oceanic dolphin family. Despite the name "whale", they are dolphins.

## Range

Orcas are found in every ocean, from the polar regions to the equator. They are
one of the most widely distributed mammals on Earth.

## Diet

Different orca populations specialize in different prey. Resident orcas mostly
eat fish such as salmon, while transient (Bigg's) orcas hunt marine mammals like
seals, sea lions, and even other whales.

## Social Structure

Orcas live in stable family groups called pods, organized around female lineage.
Knowledge and hunting techniques are passed down across generations.
